The problem

News-driven market projects often rely on copyrighted articles, noisy headlines, or unverifiable summaries. ANAS reframes the task around official documents: policy releases, central-bank communication, sanctions, trade actions, energy reports, and macro announcements.

The idea

Instead of asking a model to predict prices, ANAS asks a narrower and auditable question: what category, country, and currency exposures does this official event create, and do those exposures overlap nearby FrictionGraph chain stress?

Pipeline

From official documents to Tableau-ready event intelligence.

01

Official Sources

Fed, USTR, Treasury/OFAC, BOJ, Japan MOF, macro and policy releases.

Source selection stays license-safe by using public official documents instead of copyrighted news full text.
02

Document Store

Clean text, word-count validation, source metadata, and content hashes.

Each record keeps enough provenance to be audited later while raw document handling remains local and controlled.
03

LLM Extractor

Together AI API calls return category, country, and currency exposure vectors in strict JSON.

Together AI provides the online LLM endpoint for extraction. The model performs document-to-structure conversion rather than price prediction, producing relevance, impact, direction, and rationale fields.
04

Judge Gate

Reject malformed, weak, duplicate, or out-of-universe exposures.

A second validation layer keeps noisy outputs out of the accepted event dataset before dashboard export.
05

Alignment Export

Join accepted events with FrictionGraph RMSD and Tableau views.

The output supports explanatory inspection of event exposures near FX-chain stress windows, not causal trading claims.

Broad official-event waterfall and impact/relevance alignment ranking.

What this dashboard shows

Each point is an official public event that ANAS converted into structured category and currency exposure. The two waterfall views show where those events fall across time, while color and size encode the model-estimated impact and score.

How to read the rankings

The lower bars rank events by their overlap with nearby FrictionGraph FX-chain stress. Impact ranking favors stronger market-shock interpretation; relevance ranking favors semantic closeness even when the immediate shock is small.

Why it matters

This view is not a price prediction chart. It is an audit layer: it shows whether official macro, trade, sanctions, energy, and policy events create exposure patterns that line up with observable FX stress windows.

JPY Stress Case Study

Official BOJ/MOF events around a FrictionGraph JPY-chain stress window.

  1. Market-side signal 2025-11-28 to 2025-12-01
    FrictionGraph shows a local JPY-centered stress window. The largest standalone RMSD chain on 2025-12-01 is EUR -> SGD -> JPY -> EUR.
  2. Official-event context BOJ and Japan MOF
    ANAS identifies official BOJ speeches, Japan MOF intervention disclosures, and JGB/reserve documents as the most relevant event neighborhood for the JPY stress window.
  3. Relevance vs impact Why the split matters
    The MOF FX intervention disclosure is highly relevant to JPY but has zero immediate impact because the disclosed intervention amount is JPY 0. ANAS preserves this distinction instead of forcing a false shock narrative.
What the case isolates

The dashboard focuses on the 2025-11-28 to 2025-12-01 JPY stress window. The timeline separates the ordinary context period from the focus days so the reader can see the local jump instead of a full-year blur.

What the chain bars mean

The top-chain panel ranks the FX triangular chains contributing the most RMSD on the focus days. JPY appears inside the leading chains, which makes the official Japan-related event neighborhood worth inspecting.

What the event ranking proves

The final ranking connects official events to the JPY stress window through ANAS exposure scores. BOJ and MOF documents rise to the top, while Fed context remains visible as a broader macro backdrop.

Guardrails

ANAS is designed to be portfolio-safe and auditable.

Copyright safety

Official documents only.

The public workflow avoids storing copyrighted news article full text. Training candidates come from official public sources and derived exposure structs.

Schema control

Strict currency universe.

The current FrictionGraph alignment space is CAD, CHF, EUR, JPY, SGD, USD, with CNH and XAU as event proxies. Out-of-universe outputs are guarded.

Interpretability

No prediction claim.

Dashboards show explanatory alignment between official events and market structure stress. They are not presented as trading signals or causal proof.

Future Direction

From portfolio demo to trainable event-intelligence system.

The next step is to expand the official-event corpus from hundreds to thousands of documents while preserving the same source policy, hashing, validation, and judge pipeline. The accepted and rejected records become supervised material for a smaller task-specific model.

ANAS and FrictionGraph are designed to complement each other. FrictionGraph detects when FX market structure becomes stressed; ANAS helps explain which official event exposures belong near that stress window.
